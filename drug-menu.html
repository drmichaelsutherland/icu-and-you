Every drug covered so far, alphabetically, with a link to its profile

A short list that is meant to get long. If a drug has had a profile in the Drugstore series, it is here, and so are the brand names and class names that would send you looking for it.

Cross-references are in grey: a brand name, an alternative generic name, or a class, pointing at the profile that covers it. The profiles themselves are listed under the generic name. Drugs discussed in passing elsewhere on the site are not indexed here — this is a menu of profiles, not of mentions.
