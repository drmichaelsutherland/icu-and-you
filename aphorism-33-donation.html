ICU and You · Aphorism Series  ·  Number 33

Everyone means to be a donor. Rather fewer have decided to be one.

The distance between meaning to and having decided is about one unspent minute — the minute it takes to register a decision on the Australian Organ Donor Register.

The minute in question

This aphorism is about the distance between meaning to and having decided. The distance is about one minute, and this is it.

Anyone in Australia aged 16 or over can register, and it takes about a minute with a Medicare card. Or go to donatelife.gov.au.
