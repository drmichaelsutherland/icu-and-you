Transplantation

One word carries the ICU care of the recipient: TRANSPLANT.

T
Ischaemic timeTimes you did not control — cold and warm ischaemia shaped this graft before you met it
R
ReperfusionReperfusion syndrome — hyperkalaemia, acidosis and vasoplegia as the clamps come off
A
AnastomosesAnastomoses first — know every join, and what is lost when one of them fails
N
DenervationNerves were cut — atropine will not work, and the new lung cannot cough
S
Graft signalsSignals of function — lactate, glucose, urine output, oxygenation, hour by hour
P
PerfusionPerfusion protects the graft — no hypovolaemia, no nephrotoxins, no quiet low flow
L
ImmunosuppressionLevels daily — and again after every drug you start, stop or change
A
ProphylaxisAntimicrobial cover — valganciclovir, cotrimoxazole, antifungal; check it is actually given
N
RejectionNever one or the other — rejection and infection coexist, and biopsy decides
T
The teamTelephone the transplant unit before you change a single thing
